## 2. What data we process

**Account data:** e-mail address, login, password (stored only as an irreversible hash), first name, last name, patronymic, gender, date of birth, profile photo, "about me" text, chosen role (Client or Master), account status.

**Contacts:** phone numbers (with country code and "main" and "verified" flags); Telegram chat identifier if you connected notifications.

**Location:** addresses you provide in your profile and Listings (region, city, district, etc.). We do not track your real-time geolocation.

**Professional data (for Masters):** specialities, education, social network links, work gallery.

**External accounts:** when you sign in with Google, Facebook, Instagram or Telegram, we receive from the provider your account identifier and the profile data it shares (for example, name, e-mail address, photo).

**Your content:** Listings, uploaded photos, chat messages, reviews, complaints, support requests (for guest requests — the e-mail address for the reply), favourites, blacklist, list of recently viewed Listings (the latest 50).

**Technical data:** IP address (to limit request rates and count unique Listing views — in short-term storage, for views as a hash, about 1 hour), last-activity time (for the "online" indicator), authorisation tokens, information about your cookie consent.

**Listing change history:** when a Listing is changed, we save a "before/after" snapshot for dispute resolution and moderation; such records are available only to the administration and are kept for 14 days by default.

## 3. Why we do this

- creating and protecting an account, sign-in, e-mail confirmation, access recovery;
- operating the service: publishing Listings, search, chats, reviews, favourites, notifications;
- moderation, handling complaints and support requests, preventing fraud and abuse;
- security and stability of the Platform, request rate limiting;
- compliance with legal requirements.

Legal bases: your consent, performance of the agreement with you, the Operator's legitimate interests in keeping the Platform secure, and legal requirements.

## 4. What other users see

Your public profile and Listings show: first name, last name, patronymic, gender, photo, "about me" text, role, rating and number of reviews, specialities, addresses (to the extent you provided), social links, "online" status, your reviews and Listings.

Your e-mail address, date of birth and phone numbers are **not shown** in your public profile — only you can see them. Do not publish them yourself in Listing text or messages if you do not want to disclose them.

Chat messages are visible only to chat participants and, when a complaint or request is being reviewed, to authorised Operator staff.

## 5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by:
- **sign-in providers** — Google, Meta (Facebook, Instagram), Telegram — when you sign in through them, under their own policies;
- **e-mail and messenger services** — to send e-mails (confirmation, codes, notifications) and Telegram notifications to Operator staff and connected users;
- **hosting and infrastructure providers** on whose servers the Platform runs;
- **government authorities** — upon a justified request as provided by law.

Some of these services are located outside the Republic of Tajikistan, so data may be transferred abroad. We transfer only the necessary minimum.

## 6. Retention periods

- account and profile — while you use the Platform; after account deletion we delete or anonymise data, except what we are required by law to keep or need to protect our rights;
- unconfirmed account — may be deleted after 7 days;
- authorisation refresh token — up to 15 days; the access token is valid for about an hour;
- Listing change history — 14 days (by default);
- conversations and reviews involving other users may remain in anonymised form after account deletion;
- backups — for a limited time, then overwritten.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to: obtain information about your data, correct it (in profile settings), delete your account, withdraw consent, restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy of your data, and lodge a complaint with the competent state authority for personal data protection. To do so, write to [CONTACT E-MAIL] — we will respond within the period set by law. Withdrawing consent may make some features unavailable.

## 8. How we protect data

- passwords are stored as hashes;
- token-based authorisation; the refresh token is sent in a protected cookie (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict in production);
- connections are encrypted (HTTPS);
- limits on sign-in attempts, code requests and confirmations;
- staff access to data is restricted by roles.

No system is absolutely secure; if we detect a breach we will take measures and notify affected persons and authorities where required by law.

## 9. Cookies and local storage

We use technically necessary cookies and storage: the authorisation refresh token (protected cookie), session data and interface settings. Your cookie consent is saved in your profile. The server side of the Platform does not set third-party advertising cookies. If client applications use analytics services, we say so in the application itself. The account confirmation page may load a styling library from the jsDelivr service — in that case your IP address is visible to that service.

## 10. Age

The Platform is intended for persons **aged 18 and over**. If we learn that a minor created an account, we will delete it.
